# Study-Burnout-Prediction-System
Based on how someone studies, are they at risk of burnout?
Key Findings
High burnout students study longer hours but sleep less
Physical activity and social time reduce burnout risk
Excessive extracurricular activities increase burnout
Data-Driven Recommendations
Maintain 7–8 hours of sleep daily
Avoid studying more than 6 hours continuously
Engage in daily physical activity
Balance academics with social interaction
Limit extracurricular overload
